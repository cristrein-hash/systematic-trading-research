# Systematic Trading Research

Independent research project focused on systematic trading, market analysis, causal backtesting, risk management and AI-assisted financial workflows.

The project was developed as part of my transition into financial markets and technology, combining self-directed study with the practical development of research tools, trading models and data-driven validation workflows.

My primary research market has been **XAUUSD (Gold)**, with additional exploration across other financial instruments and market environments.

---

## Research Focus

The project explores the full process of developing and validating rule-based trading ideas, including:

- Systematic strategy development
- Historical backtesting and validation
- Market regime analysis
- Risk and trade qualification
- Price-action and market-structure research
- External macroeconomic and event factors
- Data quality and research reproducibility
- AI-assisted research workflows
- Automation of market-analysis processes

The objective is not to present a "winning strategy", but to build a disciplined framework for testing hypotheses, identifying weaknesses and improving decision-making under uncertainty.

---

## Methodology

A central principle of the research is **causal validation**.

Trading decisions are evaluated using only information that would have been available at the time of the decision.

This includes:

- Closed-bar data
- Shifted indicators when required
- Explicit prevention of look-ahead bias
- Reproducible historical tests
- Separation between research, validation and production logic
- Verification of data sources and derived features
- Risk defined before outcome evaluation

This approach became particularly important after identifying how apparently strong historical results can deteriorate significantly when hidden look-ahead effects are removed.

For me, that discovery became one of the most valuable lessons of the entire project: **research integrity matters more than impressive backtest results.**

## Selected Research Areas

### XAUUSD Systematic Research

Research into rule-based trading models for Gold across multiple timeframes, with particular focus on:

- Trend continuation
- Market structure
- Entry qualification
- Structural stop placement
- Risk/reward modelling
- Market-state filtering
- Exhaustion and extension conditions
- Historical candidate analysis

The research combines quantitative testing with structured review of market behaviour.

Specific proprietary strategy rules and parameters are intentionally not published.

---

### Market Regime & Turn-State Research

Development of a framework for classifying market conditions using only information available at each historical bar.

Research areas include:

- Bull, range and bear regimes
- Potential market turning points
- Counter-trend pullbacks
- Market-state strength and confidence
- Pivot detection latency
- Regime transitions
- Benchmark comparison

A major objective is to understand not only **what state the market is in**, but also **how quickly a systematic model can identify a meaningful transition without using future information.**

---

### External Factors Research

Development of an experimental data pipeline for incorporating information beyond price action.

Sources and research areas include:

- Macroeconomic data
- Economic calendar events
- Central-bank information
- Financial news
- Gold-related market information
- Structured event storage
- Regime-transition context

The goal is to investigate whether external information can provide independent evidence for changes already being detected in market behaviour.

## Research Workflow

A simplified version of the research process:

Market Observation
        ↓
Hypothesis
        ↓
Rule Definition
        ↓
Historical Data
        ↓
Causal Backtest
        ↓
Look-Ahead / Data Integrity Audit
        ↓
Failure Analysis
        ↓
Rule Refinement
        ↓
Out-of-Sample Validation
        ↓
Production Candidate

The workflow is intentionally iterative. Failed hypotheses and invalidated results are treated as useful research outcomes rather than hidden or discarded.
